Hardware Bottleneck Assumption

The belief that CPU/GPU lag is the *primary* driver of your 10-hour editing sessions.

Substitution Bias

Swapping the complex task of "fixing my workflow" for the simple task of "buying a product."

Ostrich Effect

Ignoring how much time is spent on non-technical tasks like clip selection and indecision.

The Lean Workflow Method

Phase 1

Root Cause Audit

  • ⏱️ Keep a 15-minute increment log for one full edit.
  • 📊 Separate A-Roll, B-Roll, and System Latency.
Phase 2

Process Fixes

  • 📄 Tighten scripts before filming to reduce "bad takes."
  • ⚡ Learn Proxy Editing to remove lag on current hardware.
Phase 3

Constraint Test

  • ⚖️ Apply the 80/20 rule: limit to 3 cuts per minute.
  • 💰 Protect savings for content/review units.
